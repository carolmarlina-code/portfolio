Podcast promotion

Relaunching the CEO’s podcast

  • Sep 2024 – Mar 2025
The brief

Jon Michail’s Personal Branding Masterclass had a back catalogue of more than 150 episodes. The goal was to relaunch the show and reach listeners beyond Jon’s existing followers.

Events and partnerships

Sponsor pitches, orientation decks and team briefings

  • Monash University Malaysia, SASS
  • NGOPI, Indonesian Day Out
  • 2023 – 2024
The brief

These two volunteer roles were where I learned events communication by doing it. As Events Officer for the School of Arts and Social Sciences (SASS) at Monash University Malaysia, I was responsible for securing partners for a Canva and Public Speaking workshop and for making new students feel at home from their first week.

A year later in Melbourne, I brought that experience to the events department of the NGOPI Charity Ball, which ran the ball’s pre-event, Indonesian Day Out 2024, entirely on its own. Beyond the prospectus, I designed the workshop deck, two orientation decks and an arcade-style deck for Mingle with SASS, a game night for new students, each with its own visual identity.

For Indonesian Day Out, I wrote, designed and presented a technical meeting deck for each sport, covering the double-elimination bracket, match rules, dos and don’ts and a live team draw.

Result

Part four

University research and writing

My degrees are in international studies and journalism, so much of my writing is research-led. Below is a team project for an industry client, followed by selected essays and reporting.

Research, pitch and design

A cost-of-living proposal for a civic engagement platform

  • OurVoice (industry client)
  • Monash team project
  • Research, pitch design and web direction
  • 2025
The brief

OurVoice runs a platform that helps people’s views reach government decision-makers. Our team of seven was asked to propose how it could increase public participation in policymaking on the cost-of-living crisis.

What I did

I helped research the problem and gather the evidence behind our proposal, from a survey of 144 students on the initiatives they wanted most to interviews with people feeling the pressure of rising costs. I designed the entire pitch deck, turning that research into charts and quotes a panel could take in at a glance, and helped present it.

Beyond the pitch, I briefed the website development team on how the OurVoice site should look, so it reflected the platform’s values and stayed consistent with our vision and visual style. The mockups show how each proposal would run as a live poll the public could vote on.

Research behind it

144 students surveyedplus 11 interviews and ABS and OECD data. The team worked with an OurVoice industry partner and an academic coach.
